Hello world! 

My name is Crystal and I’ve been practicing pottery for approximately three years, and hopefully many more! I love the feeling of seeing a finished piece and knowing the work that took to get it to that stage. Pottery has also taught me a lot about patience and giving up perfectionism and control over things we cannot control. 

The purpose of this space is to share my pottery, and my life outside of pottery, which mostly revolves around language learning, muay thai and traveling. 

This year, I will be moving to Japan for a year and during this time, I hope to document my exploration into Japanese ceramics, muay thai/martial arts and the Japanese language.

### Process & Materials

Every piece is crafted in my home studio. I formulate my own glazes using locally sourced ash and minerals, resulting in surfaces that are unique to my environment. The firing process is a collaboration with the kiln; I can guide the heat, but the flame has the final say.

When I am not in the studio, you can find me hiking the trails of the Cascades or scouring the beach for interesting stones and driftwood.
